# B0 Onboarding

**Week 1 · licence L0 · 2 modules, about 3 sessions**

## Goals
1. **Why am I here?** The tutor bot interviews you about your goals, interests, experience and dream projects. Together you pick a **track** (your theme for the year), and you can change it later.
2. **Everything works.** GitHub account, your private learning repo, cs50.dev, the Telegram tutor, all with privacy settings right from the start.
3. **The deal.** Five ground rules, and how the course works: hearts, the AI licence ladder, the weekly digest.

## Why it matters
Self-paced courses fail mostly on motivation, not content ([research report](../../docs/design/research-report.md), key finding 4). A clear "why", a project you care about and a weekly ritual are the strongest protection. Getting privacy right on day one (the noreply email, no personal details in the repo) is much easier than fixing it later.

## Exit
- `about-me.md` in your repo: goals, track hypothesis, experience notes. **No personal details**: no real name, age, school, town or photos.
- Ground rules agreed with your parent or mentor.

## Licence
L0 (Tutor) throughout. The bot explains and asks questions; it doesn't write code for you.

## Skip test
None. Onboarding is for everyone. If you already have the accounts, M0.2 takes 20 minutes. The interview also flags skip tests for later blocks (B1, B3, M2.1) if you already know the material.
